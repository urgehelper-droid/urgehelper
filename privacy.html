Urge Helper

Privacy

Effective October 23, 2026

Everything you put into Urge Helper stays on your device. We don’t have a server that stores it, we don’t track you, and we don’t sell anything.

What’s stored, and where

If you create an account or use the reset, these are saved in your browser’s local storage on the device you’re using:

  • Your first name and email address
  • Your promise, what you wrote about what it’s costing you, and the person you chose to text
  • Your journal entries, including any slips you log
  • Session history: when you used the reset, how strong the urge was before and after, what set it off, any of the urge’s excuses you tapped, which steps you finished, and your plan

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We can’t see it, and we couldn’t hand it over if asked, because we never receive it. The flip side: if you clear your browser data or switch devices, it’s gone, and we can’t recover it.

Signing in with Google

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and an account ID with this page so it can recognise you on this device. That information is stored in the same local storage described above and isn’t sent to a server of ours. Google’s sign-in service is only contacted if you tap that button. Google’s own privacy policy covers what Google does.

What we don’t do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No microphone, camera or location access.

Other services that see basic connection data

  • Our hosting provider serves the site and, like any web host, may keep short-lived server logs that include your IP address.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Deleting your data

Open your profile, tap your initial, and choose Delete all my data. That removes your profile, pledge, journal and history from the device. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Not medical advice

Urge Helper is a self-help tool built on published research. It isn’t medical or mental health treatment. If urges feel compulsive or distressing, a CBT or ACT therapist has the strongest evidence. If you’re in crisis, you can call or text 988, any time.

Children

Urge Helper isn’t intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change how data is handled, we’ll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
