Your promise

For the moment an urge hits.

Urge Helper is a free, private place to go when a porn urge hits. One tap starts a session of about 10 to 15 minutes. It asks how strong the urge is and what set it off, then picks the steps that fit: getting away from the trigger, moving your body, a game that crowds the urge out, riding out what’s left, and a plan for next time. Every step comes from published research.

Urges build, peak and fade. You don’t have to fight harder. You just have to get to the other side of it.

How to stop a porn urge in the moment

  1. Name how strong it is. Rate the urge from 0 to 10. Putting a number on it takes away a little of its power, and it shows you later how much it dropped.
  2. Step away. Close what you were looking at and leave the room, ideally to somewhere with light or other people. Being alone in bed with your phone is the classic risky moment.
  3. Name what set it off. Stress, boredom, loneliness, a bad mood, being tired, or something you saw. Your trigger decides which steps help most.
  4. Give your body and mind something else to do. Pushups or a brisk walk, slow breathing (in for four, out for six), or a game that keeps your hands and imagination busy.
  5. Ride it out. Watch the urge rise, peak and fall without acting on it. This is called urge surfing.
  6. Make a plan for next time. Decide now, while your head is clear: “If I’m bored with nothing on, I’ll do 20 pushups.”

Why urges pass

An urge feels like it will last until you give in, but it doesn’t. Cravings come in waves that usually peak and fade within about 15 to 20 minutes. Every time you ride one out without acting on it, the habit gets a little weaker.

Common questions

Is Urge Helper free?
Yes. No ads, no subscription, no payment of any kind.
Do I need an account?
No. You can start a session right away. Signing in only lets you keep your promise, journal and progress on this device.
Is it private?
Yes. There are no trackers or cookies. The only thing counted is two anonymous totals: how many people have visited and how many pressed Help me now. Your promise, journal and history stay in your own browser.
How long does a session take?
About 10 to 15 minutes, a little longer if the urge is strong. You can keep going if it hasn’t passed.
Does it work?
Each technique has research behind it, mostly from studies on porn use, cravings and habits. The full list is under Science. This exact sequence hasn’t been tested as a whole.
Is this therapy?
No. It’s a tool for the moment an urge hits. If urges feel compulsive or distressing, a therapist who does CBT or ACT has the strongest evidence.
